# Eden Musk product-label edits

Created with the built-in image generation tool from the original retailer bottle photographs. Each edit preserves its perfume number, bottle style and second reference bottle, replaces the main label with EDEN MUSK, and removes unsupported promotional badges. These are digitally relabeled packaging visualizations; source specifications do not establish manufactured Eden Musk inventory.

Files follow `eden-musk-NUMBER.webp`. `data/branded-photos.js` maps stable source IDs to the images. They are used throughout the shop, search, favorites, quiz, comparison, bundles, bag and source library. Unrelated retailer promotional slides are not displayed as Eden Musk offers. Non-perfume reference listings use Eden Musk collection artwork.

The original generated PNGs were exported to WebP without changing their dimensions.

Prompt template (NUMBER is the matching perfume number):

> Edit this product photograph precisely. On the front main clear perfume bottle with a black cylindrical cap, replace the printed brand SCENTORA with EDEN MUSK in clean dark elegant uppercase lettering. Preserve product number NUMBER exactly and prominently below EDEN MUSK. Keep the bottle silhouette, cap, glass, liquid color, cream label style, camera angle, second reference bottle at right, background, shadows and reflections. Remove BEST SELLER / COMPLIMENT GETTER promotional icons and slogans by restoring the background. Do not add marketing claims. Do not change the product number or alter the second reference bottle. The main bottle label must visibly read EDEN MUSK above NUMBER, with EXTRAIT DE PARFUM in small text at the bottom if present. No SCENTORA or ALT wording should remain on the main bottle. Realistic premium product photograph, square image.
